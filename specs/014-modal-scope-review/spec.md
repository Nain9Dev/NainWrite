# Modal Scope Review

- **SCOPE-001:** When aligned source and candidate sentences preserve their order
  but a source conditional becomes unqualified ability, a conditional in another
  sentence shall not suppress the deterministic review finding.
- **SCOPE-002:** When multiple conditional expressions become fewer possibility
  expressions alongside unqualified ability, the checker shall retain a finding
  even if sentence splitting differs. This is a conservative review signal, not
  a proof that every counted expression has the same referent.
- **SCOPE-003:** When `May` occurs in a recognised English date expression, it
  shall not count as a modal verb. Genuine modal `may`, code/quote exclusions and
  unchanged compatible conditionals shall remain supported.
- **SCOPE-004:** When fidelity logic changes, generation, review and evidence
  replay shall use the same versioned rule. Existing jobs shall not silently
  inherit the new configuration; prompt size and existing literal checks remain.

No new dependency, service, download or model is required `[A]`. Exact final
document approval remains `[M]`. Sentence alignment is positional and deliberately
limited; abbreviations, reordering and complex paraphrase may require human review.
